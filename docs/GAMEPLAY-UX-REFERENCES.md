# Dungeon playability review scenarios

Revision: 2026-10-15. Internal acceptance scenarios derived from the supplied GDD, not external research or evidence of implemented playability. Screenshots illustrate composition; they do not validate balance.

## First access

A new player can identify Start Endless, understand typing-to-dash and begin with keyboard only. The castle and knight visibly live before start; sound does not autoplay without a gesture. No unexplained damage, modal stack or mandatory account interrupts the first encounter.

## Targeting and pressure

Show several enemies and choose a word by its first letter. Confirm an unmistakable target lock, correct case handling, stable prefix through enemy motion, no intersecting labels and no word hidden under HUD. Test duplicate prefixes and a long Indonesian elite label on a 320px viewport. Check the queue actually prioritizes a dangerous bomber over a distant low-risk mob.

## Tab interruption

Partially type an elite word, enter Tab mode, cast Cleave, and return to exactly the same prefix. Nearby trash dies; remaining elite layers decrease without rewriting the current word. Try insufficient mana, cooldown, Tab cancellation and a simultaneous level-up/death transition. Only completed arena words reduce cooldown.

## Build and progression

Earn XP by kills/pickups, reach the 50-character magnet and confirm real gem movement/rewards. Make an error and verify streak reset. Level up, choose each offer family by keyword, check tactical freeze and overflow levels. Long-run draft exhaustion must still offer valid choices.

## Boss and endless continuation

Survive to the five-minute schedule, strip boss armor, and type through the ten-second stagger. Verify real per-character damage and embedded ultimate. Test both boss survival/armor rebuild and boss death/relic/biome. Continue past the boss and beyond 15 minutes; death/restart remains reachable.

## Safe training and multiplayer

Dojo preserves editable typing, EN/ID, symbols/digits and useful settings without survival damage. Castle Sprint uses four real clients and obstacle actions affecting laps/results. Bridge Clash uses actual attack/defense state. Raid uses real party healing, tank behavior and shared revives. No decorative opponents count as multiplayer.

## Lifecycle and accessibility

Pause during dash/cast/stagger; open settings/auth; leave and re-enter every mode. Prefix, timer and focus behave predictably. Tab navigation works outside combat. Check touch/native composition input does not double-submit. Context recovery, hidden-tab return, Reduced Effects and saved-run resume cannot produce catch-up damage or uncontrolled allocations.

## Review record

Save actual implementation screenshots, test reports and hardware/profile details when these scenarios are run. Do not reuse old fishing screenshots or claim screenshot similarity proves the game is playable.
